MAIE, directly.

You already know the problem you're trying to solve. This page skips the story and shows you MAIE plainly: what it is, who it's for, what works today, and what it costs.

MAIE is a production memory system for creative teams, built by MAIE Intelligence Technologies LLC. It keeps a project's media, research and history in one private workspace, so the work you've already done stays findable, understandable and reusable.

Who is MAIE for?

MAIE starts with the people who make media on small budgets and tight timelines. Pick the one closest to you.

  • Independent filmmakers & music-video directors"I know the shot exists. I just can't find it."
  • Small studios & production teams"More projects, without more people chasing files."
  • Students, educators & media programs"Learn how real productions keep track of their work."

Video editors, content creators and media archives: dedicated pages are on the way, and the capabilities below apply to you too.

What problem does MAIE solve?

Every production leaves a trail: takes, drafts, references, renamed files, notes, decisions. Most of it ends up scattered across drives, phones, shared folders and people's memories. By the next project, or the next editor, much of it is effectively gone. Files named v1_final_FINAL.mov are the symptom. The cost is the hours spent re-finding, re-watching and re-asking.

MAIE's job is to make that history stay with the project.

How does MAIE work?

  1. Start a project. Every project is its own private workspace. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
  2. Bring your media in. For images, MAIE flags likely duplicates (the same file from more than one source, or near-identical shots) so you can keep the one you want instead of letting copies pile up.
  3. MAIE reads what you bring. It writes a plain-language description of images and finds the scene boundaries in video: the raw material of a searchable log, without logging by hand.
  4. Keep your research with the work. Save references and articles from MAIE's discovery feed into a project, where they become searchable by meaning, not just by keyword.
  5. Hand off the busywork. Describe the outcome you want, and MAIE's agent drafts a plan you can review before it runs.
