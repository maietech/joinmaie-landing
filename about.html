About MAIE

MAIE is the creative companion for modern media. The endless folders, the lost takes, the missing metadata — managed, mapped, and silently understood. MAIE carries your media from human impulse to finished artifact, so you can spend more time creating and less time organizing.

What MAIE Does

  • Every file remembered. MAIE recognizes every file you bring in, keeps track of every edit, and never lets a duplicate slip through.
  • Your work stays yours. Every project lives in its own private space. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
  • Your progress, never lost. Your past work is always there, exactly as you left it, even as MAIE keeps growing and improving.

MAIE & Midwest Media Alliance

MAIE and Midwest Media Alliance share one vision from two different directions: MAIE builds the technology, Midwest Media Alliance carries that craft to media professionals and creative communities across the Midwest. Learn more at Midwest Media Alliance.

MAIE is built by MAIE Intelligence Technologies LLC. MAIE is in active development — for questions, partnership inquiries, or anything else, see Contact.
